## Information We Collect

### 1. Local Data Storage

The App stores the following data **locally on your device only**:
- **High Scores**: Your top scores for each game mode (Normal, Hard, Super Hard)
- **Game Statistics**: Number of games played, yaku formed
- **Settings**: Sound preferences

**Important**: This data is stored locally using device storage (localStorage) and is **never transmitted to external servers**.

### 2. Advertising Data

The App displays advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect and process certain information for advertising purposes:

#### Data Collected by AdMob:
- **Device Information**: Device model, operating system version
- **Advertising Identifier (IDFA)**: Used for ad targeting and measurement (only if you consent to tracking)
- **Usage Data**: App interactions, ad impressions, ad clicks
- **Approximate Location**: Based on IP address (not precise GPS location)

#### Purpose of Data Collection:
- Display relevant advertisements
- Measure ad performance
- Prevent fraud and improve ad quality

#### AdMob's Privacy Policy:
For detailed information about how Google AdMob collects and uses data, please review:
- Google AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google's Partner Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

### 3. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

**The App does NOT request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission.**

This means:
- We do NOT track you across apps and websites owned by other companies
- AdMob uses only **non-personalized ads** or **contextual advertising**
- Your advertising identifier is NOT used for cross-app tracking

## Your Rights and Choices

### Control Your Data:
1. **Delete Local Data**: Delete the app to remove all locally stored data
2. **Limit Ad Tracking**: 
   - iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
   - iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → Disable "Personalized Ads"
3. **Opt-Out of Interest-Based Ads**: Visit Google's Ad Settings at https://adssettings.google.com

### Data Access:
Since all game data is stored locally on your device, you have full control. You can:
- View your high scores in the app
- Clear data by deleting the app
- Export data is not currently supported (all data is local)
