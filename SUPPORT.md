# Mahjong Drop - Support

## About the Game

Mahjong Drop is a fast-paced puzzle game that combines falling tile action with authentic Japanese Mahjong scoring. Collect 14 tiles within the time limit and score points based on your winning combinations (yaku).

## How to Play

1. **Start the Game**: Choose your difficulty mode (Normal, Hard, or Super Hard)
2. **Collect Tiles**: Tap falling Mahjong tiles to collect them
3. **Complete Your Hand**: Collect exactly 14 tiles before time runs out
4. **Score Points**: Your hand is automatically evaluated for winning combinations (yaku)

## Game Modes

- **Normal Mode**: 60 seconds, moderate speed (1.5x)
- **Hard Mode**: 45 seconds, faster pace (2.5x)
- **Super Hard Mode**: 30 seconds, extreme challenge (3.5x)

## Mahjong Yaku (Winning Combinations)

The game evaluates your 14-tile hand using authentic Japanese Mahjong rules. Here are some common yaku:

### Basic Yaku (1-2 han)
- **All Simples (Tanyao)** - Only tiles 2-8, no terminals or honors
- **Pinfu** - Four sequences and a non-value pair
- **Pure Double Sequence (Iipeikou)** - Two identical sequences
- **Value Tile (Yakuhai)** - Triplet of dragons (Haku/Hatsu/Chun) or your wind
- **Seven Pairs (Chiitoitsu)** - Seven pairs of identical tiles

### Intermediate Yaku (2-3 han)
- **All Triplets (Toitoi)** - Four triplets and a pair
- **Three Concealed Triplets (San Ankou)** - Three triplets
- **Mixed Terminals (Honroutou)** - Only terminals (1,9) and honors
- **Straight (Ikkitsukan)** - Same suit 123, 456, 789 sequences
- **Mixed Triple Sequence (Sanshoku Doujun)** - Same number sequence in all three suits

### Advanced Yaku (3-6 han)
- **Half Flush (Honitsu)** - One suit + honors only
- **Twice Pure Double Sequence (Ryanpeikou)** - Two pairs of identical sequences
- **Full Flush (Chinitsu)** - One suit only, no honors

### Yakuman (13 han)
- **Thirteen Orphans (Kokushi Musou)** - All terminals and honors
- **Big Three Dragons (Daisangen)** - Triplets of all three dragons
- **All Honors (Tsuuiisou)** - Only honor tiles
- **Four Concealed Triplets (Suuankou)** - Four concealed triplets
- **Pure Terminals (Chinroutou)** - Only 1s and 9s

## Tips for High Scores

1. **Aim for Flush Yaku**: Collecting tiles of the same suit can lead to high-scoring Full Flush or Half Flush
2. **Look for Sequences**: Consecutive numbered tiles are easier to form into sequences
3. **Don't Ignore Honors**: Dragon triplets can add valuable han to your hand
4. **Practice Different Modes**: Each mode offers unique challenges and scoring opportunities
5. **Learn Common Yaku**: Familiarize yourself with basic yaku patterns to recognize scoring opportunities

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Do I need to know Mahjong to play?
**A:** No! The game is easy to pick up. Just tap tiles and the game automatically evaluates your hand. However, knowledge of Mahjong yaku will help you aim for higher scores.

### Q: What happens if I don't collect 14 tiles in time?
**A:** The game ends and you score 0 points. Try an easier difficulty mode to practice.

### Q: Can I see which yaku I formed?
**A:** Yes! After collecting 14 tiles, the result screen shows all yaku you formed and the total score.

### Q: How is the score calculated?
**A:** Scores are based on the total han (番) of all yaku in your hand. More han = higher score. The game uses simplified scoring based on han count.

### Q: Why does the game show "No Yaku"?
**A:** Your 14 tiles don't form any valid Mahjong winning combinations. In Mahjong, you need at least one yaku to score points, even if you have a complete hand (4 melds + 1 pair).
