How to Play

Basic Rules

Mahjong tiles fall from the top of the screen.

Tap 14 tiles before time runs out to complete your hand.

Scoring

Once you collect 14 tiles, your hand is automatically evaluated.

Points are awarded based on the winning combinations (yaku) you form.

No valid yaku = 0 points.

Example Yaku

Seven Pairs (2 han): 7 pairs of identical tiles
All Simples (1 han): Only tiles 2–8, no terminals or honors
Pinfu (1 han): 4 sequences + a non-value pair
Full Flush (6 han): All tiles of one suit, no honors

Tips

· Collecting tiles of the same suit makes yaku easier to form

· Honor tiles can form Value Tile yaku (triplets)

· Aim for consecutive numbered tiles to build sequences
